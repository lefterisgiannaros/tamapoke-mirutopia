TamaPoke

Gift build 3.13.0 — 809 Pokémon (Kanto–Alola), splash, Prof. Tangrowth, walk bonus, SD save backup. Local installer — Chrome or Edge.

Your browser doesn't support Web Serial. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Board not showing up?

A brand-new board may need download mode:

hold BOOT → tap RESET → let go of BOOT

Use a data USB-C cable, straight into the computer.

1Flash the firmware

Plug the board in via USB and press install. Takes about a minute.

Browser not supported (use Chrome/Edge). Needs HTTPS.

Updating? Leave "Erase device" unchecked. The creature, Pokédex and badges live in flash NVS. Tick erase and that is gone. The microSD also keeps /saves/latest.tkps — step 3 can put it back.

A fresh board can be erased safely.

2Load the sprites onto the microSD

Put a microSD into the board. Press Connect board, then a region. Kanto first is fine; add others later.

Kanto is ~40 MB and takes about 10–15 minutes over USB. Leave the tab open.

Close the step-1 install tab first (only one program can use the port).

Have your own .bin / .rgb files?

After Connect: Push splash writes /splash.rgb, Push professor writes /tangrowth.rgb. Then restart.

How to play

  
Care for your egg until it hatches, feed it, play, train it and complete them
     all. Tap the creature to pet it; swipe to open the Pokédex (sideways), the
     stat card (up) or set the clock (down); the physical PWR button turns the
     screen off (short press) or everything off (long press).
